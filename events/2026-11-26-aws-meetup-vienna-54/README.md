# 2026-11-26-aws-meetup-vienna-54

AWS Meetup Vienna #54, 26 November 2026, 5 PM, hosted by Erste Digital in the
Zeta Bite room, Canettistraße 5, 1100 Wien.

- `source.json`: the Meetup link and the overrides (the sponsor logo, the two
  speakers with their photos and roles, `links.nextEvents`, and talk 2's
  abstract without the `Sponsors:` block the page carries at its end)
- `event.json`, `meetup.json`: generated by `make sync`, do not edit
- `assets/`: the speaker photos and the Erste Digital logo (PNG beside the SVG,
  because the deck needs a PNG)
- `canva/header.png`, `canva/talk-1.png`, `canva/talk-2.png`: the announcement
  cards, 1920 x 1080, in the #53 layout; the deck uses them
- `deck/header.jpeg`: the header at 1600 x 900, for the deck's header slide
- `canva/source/`: the venue photo, the Erste Digital logo, the two build
  scripts and `SOURCES.md`

The files in `assets/` are used with their owners' agreement: each speaker
agreed to their own photo, Erste Digital to their logo for the meetup they
host. Their entries for the consent ledger (#1) are the two photos as `asked`
and the logo as `sponsor`.
